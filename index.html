Move 1: Establishing Territory

Claiming centrality and/or making topic generalizations

🎯 Key Strategies

  • Claiming centrality
  • Making topic generalizations
  • Reviewing items of previous research

📝 Linguistic Signals

  • Recently, researchers have become increasingly interested in...
  • Many studies have shown that...
  • It is generally accepted that...

⚠️ Common Errors

  • Making claims without evidence
  • Failing to demonstrate topic importance
  • Overgeneralizing previous research

📊 Contrastive Examples

✅ Effective:

"In recent years, climate change has emerged as a critical area of research, with numerous studies documenting its impact on coastal ecosystems (Smith, 2020; Jones, 2021)."

⭐ Demonstrates centrality with evidence
❌ Ineffective:

"Climate change is happening. People study it."

⚠️ Vague, lacks academic rigor
