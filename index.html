📝 Interactive Example

Original: "This paper is about online learning."

CARS Improved: "Recent years have witnessed growing scholarly interest in online learning environments (Territory). However, few studies have examined their long-term effectiveness for skill retention (Niche). This study therefore investigates the impact of online micro-learning modules on six-month knowledge retention (Occupation)."

🃏 CARS Model Flashcards

What is the purpose of Move 1 (Territory) in the CARS model?

To establish the importance and centrality of the research topic, showing readers why it matters and situating it within existing scholarship.
